Current focus

Section 2

Human + AI decision-making

AI is both something I investigate and something that is changing how I investigate and make.

My recent work explores AI from both sides of the design process. I research how people work with increasingly capable AI — how they interrogate evidence, establish trust and retain judgement and accountability.

At the same time, AI is changing how I research and design. The boundary between designing and building is becoming more fluid, allowing questions to move from research, through design and into working software. For me, this is part of a familiar evolution in design tools. What matters is still the judgement about what to build, what not to build, and what needs to exist to answer the question.

Design as research

Section 5

Research doesn’t end with a report.

Research can take many forms, and it doesn’t stop when designing or making begins. Mapping relationships, sketching an idea, staging an encounter or building working software can all be ways of investigating a question. What I make — and how I make it — depends on what I’m trying to understand.

PhD in Design

Section 6

A practice-based PhD in embodied and somatic design

My research starts with people whose practices are deeply embodied. I work with practitioners from disciplines such as theatre and climbing to explore what their ways of working can teach us about design, and how thinking about design activities as encounters might change the way we understand embodied experience.
