## Abstract

This article, which appeared in Theatre, Dance and Performance Training, Volume 10, Issue 2 (2019), investigates how digital technologies can be used to enhance the relational aspects of performer training. Saner and Robinson reflect on a practice as research project, *Enactive Encounters*, where they use poor technology and everyday objects to create participatory learning environments. The teacher–student relationship is challenged and transformed into playful interactions between participants through enactive encounters that aim to embody different aspects of specific training practices.
